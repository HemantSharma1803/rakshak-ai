      THE IDEA

      A scam is a script.
      Rakshak marks the script.

      Instead of asking you to trust a mysterious score, Rakshak points at the exact words creating urgency, borrowing authority, demanding secrecy, extracting money or credentials, or moving you to an unsafe channel.

      01

      Detect the move

      Signals cover pressure, fear, authority, secrecy, OTP/PIN requests, advance fees, prize and job bait, remote-access apps, shortened links and lookalike domains.

      02

      Show the evidence

      Triggered phrases are highlighted in the original message. Each finding carries an explanation and the text that caused it.

      03

      Give the next action

      Risky results turn into a short checklist: do not engage, verify through a trusted channel, and report financial loss quickly.

      04

      Keep the message private

      The app is static JavaScript. There is no login, backend, analytics, database, or API key. Analysis happens in the browser.

      IMPORTANT LIMIT

      Rakshak is a reading aid, not a truth machine.

      A clean result does not prove that a sender or link is legitimate. Never share an OTP, PIN or password because a message tells you to. For money already lost in India, use the official cybercrime reporting route or call 1930.
